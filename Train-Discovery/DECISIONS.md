# Gaze — audit and decisions

This file records what the codebase already had when the Gaze addendum started (2026-10-25), and every ambiguous decision taken while building it. When a choice was open, the simplest option that keeps existing behavior working was picked.

## 2. Decisions

### Scope and process

- **D1. One branch, built in sequence.** The spec suggests parallel worktrees. Every feature integrates through the same files (`discovery-app.tsx`, `globals.css`, the audio hook), so parallel branches would conflict on almost every merge. Features are still separate modules behind flags in `src/lib/features.ts`, built on the `gaze` branch, in the spec's phase order.
- **D2. Folder and package names stay.** The directory remains `Train-Discovery/` (CI and local paths depend on it) and `package.json` keeps `"name": "sbuhacks"`. Only the product name changes.

### Rebrand

- **D3. Storage keys and caches are renamed with migration.** `gaze:ride:v1`, `gaze:bookmarks:v1`, `gaze:settings:v1`. Old `train-discovery:*` keys are read once, migrated, and removed. The service worker cache prefix becomes `gaze-`, and old `train-discovery-` caches are deleted on activate.
- **D4. Brand mark.** The train-front icon is replaced by a compass mark: steel hairline ring, brass needle. This is the one place brass appears (the spec reserves brass for the compass needle; the ride instrument is the map, so the needle lives only in the mark and icons).

### Network

- **D5. Networks are config.** `src/networks/{types,lirr,california-zephyr,index}.ts`. A network owns its rail data, rides, demo start, jump targets, map bounds, and attribution. `california-zephyr` is the default. `lirr` stays in the codebase with `enabled: false`: it is not offered in the UI, but `/?network=lirr` loads it as a developer override (like `/?dev`), which also keeps the existing LIRR browser tests meaningful.
- **D6. Zephyr segments are the "routes".** The existing app selects one `RailRoute` and rides it. Each Zephyr segment becomes one `RailRoute` (`cz-plains`, `cz-rockies`, `cz-canyons`, `cz-basin`, `cz-sierra`, `cz-bay`), so selection, replay, station announcements, and pointing keep working unchanged. The picker lists every segment; only `cz-rockies` is marked "Full experience". Other segments have stations and announcements but no POI catalog.
- **D7. Zephyr data is a static route package, not Supabase.** Supabase's `places` / `rail_routes` constraints and `refresh_catalog` are LIRR-specific (13 routes). Rather than migrate the live database during a hackathon, the Zephyr catalog ships as JSON in `public/data/networks/california-zephyr/` and `GET /api/journeys/cz-*` serves it. This also means the demo does not depend on the database. `/api/audio` and `/api/photos` look up Zephyr places in the package first.
- **D8. Route source.** Amtrak's published GTFS (`https://content.amtrak.com/content/gtfs/GTFS.zip`, feed version 20261024) has route 96 "California Zephyr" with shapes 173 (westbound) and 174 (eastbound). Shape 173 passes within 14–81 m of every Rockies and Sierra station, with a 65 m median point spacing between Denver and Glenwood Springs, so no OSM/NTAD fallback is needed. Verified stop IDs: DEN, WIP, GRA, GSC, RNO, TRU, RSV. Westbound (Chicago → Emeryville) is the **canonical direction**: `routeKm` grows westward and `signedLateralM > 0` means left of a westbound passenger facing forward.
- **D9. Route color.** The GTFS color `#CAE4F1` is nearly invisible on the canvas `#eaedf3`. The line is drawn in a darker tone of the same hue as route data.

### Content and providers

- **D10. ElevenLabs budget.** The key's remaining quota cannot be read, and the app ceiling is 9,000 characters/month. Story bodies for a short list of demo POIs are pre-generated as static MP3s (`public/audio/demo/`) under a character budget enforced by `scripts/precompute-demo.ts`. Short navigation lines (look-ahead cues, warnings) use the device voice; they are templates with live values (ETA), so pre-generating them would multiply cost. Everything without a clip falls back to device speech in the active language, with the existing visible notice.
- **D11. Narration is composed as segments.** For Zephyr places, a spoken moment is an opener (context lead, side cue, or rotating intro) followed by the verified story body and an optional look-for line. The body can be a narrator clip; the opener and look-for line are short templates. LIRR keeps its existing single-clip flow.

### Product rules carried forward

- **D12. The left/right rule changes.** `PRODUCT.md` said "no left/right window selector". The addendum asks for side cues and a window-side advisor, which supersedes that. Side labels come from route geometry only and never claim the place is visible.
- **D13. Zephyr narration plays static clips or the device voice.** The client composes each spoken line (lead, story body, look-for cue) and plays a pre-generated narrator clip when one exists for those exact words (`src/lib/clip-key.ts`); otherwise it uses the device voice in the rider's language. Live ElevenLabs generation for Zephyr places is behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_FEATURE_LIVE_NARRATION` (off), so rehearsals cannot burn the monthly budget. LIRR keeps its live, cached pipeline.
- **D14. Zephyr places come from Wikidata, linear features from OpenStreetMap.** overpass-api.de reset TLS connections all night and the mail.ru mirror times out (504) on anything but small queries, so the importer finds places with Wikidata SPARQL (`wikibase:around` every 5 km, 6.5 km radius) and uses Overpass only for rail tunnels, rivers, and canyons in 40 km chunks (cached; a failed chunk is skipped and can be rerun). Curated places need an English Wikipedia article, a heritage designation, or a spec seed. Modern RTD transit stops are demoted (their intros say RTD/light rail, with no Amtrak mention, no pre-1950 date, and no Zephyr stop within 400 m). Buildings over 1.2 km from the track need 25+ Wikipedia editions. At most three curated places per 3 km. Result: 48 curated places (not 60; the remaining candidates were downtown Denver buildings out of view or unsourced) and 90 named places for passing mentions.
- **D15. "Both sides" reach.** A canyon's or town's Wikidata point can sit hundreds of meters off the track while the train runs through it, so canyons and valleys count as "both sides" within 1.2 km, towns within 700 m, rivers within 300 m; everything else is "ahead" within 150 m.
